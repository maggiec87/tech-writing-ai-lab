AI Product & UX explores how AI-powered products are discovered, designed, evaluated, and improved from the perspective of users and product teams.

Unlike traditional software, AI products often produce probabilistic outputs, respond differently to similar inputs, and introduce new interaction patterns. These characteristics create challenges in product definition, user experience, usability, trust, and quality evaluation.

This area focuses on understanding user needs, analyzing AI product experiences, and applying product and UX methods to improve how people interact with AI.

## What This Area Covers

- **AI Product Fundamentals:** Understanding AI product characteristics, opportunities, constraints, and product strategy.
- **Product Discovery:** Identifying user problems, researching needs, and evaluating opportunities for AI solutions.
- **User Research:** Understanding user behavior, expectations, workflows, and pain points.
- **Product Requirements:** Translating user needs and product goals into clear requirements and acceptance criteria.
- **AI Product Lifecycle:** Exploring discovery, definition, design, development, evaluation, launch, and iteration.
- **AI UX Design:** Designing interactions for AI-powered features and applications.
- **Human-AI Interaction:** Understanding collaboration, user control, feedback, and appropriate levels of automation.
- **Conversational UX:** Designing effective interactions for chatbots and conversational interfaces.
- **Feedback and Error Recovery:** Helping users understand AI outputs, correct mistakes, and recover from failures.
- **Trust and Transparency:** Communicating AI capabilities, limitations, uncertainty, and data usage.
- **Responsible AI:** Considering privacy, fairness, safety, accessibility, and user well-being.
- **AI Product Evaluation:** Assessing usability, usefulness, output quality, and overall user experience.

## How This Area Is Organized

This topic is explored through three complementary approaches.

### Learn

Build a systematic understanding of AI product development, user research, interaction design, and evaluation methods.

Topics include:

- Evaluating AI Products from a User Perspective
- Evaluating AI Chatbots
- AI Product Fundamentals
- AI Product Lifecycle
- AI Product Requirements
- AI UX Design Principles
- Human-AI Interaction
- Conversational UX Design
- Designing AI Feedback and Error Recovery
- Trust, Transparency, and Responsible AI

Further topics may include AI product discovery, usability testing, AI product metrics, and experimentation.

### Reviews

Study real AI products and analyze how their product decisions and user experiences address practical user needs.

These case studies go beyond listing product features. They examine the target users, use cases, interaction patterns, onboarding, feedback mechanisms, output quality, trust, and limitations.

Potential case study areas include:

- AI Chatbot Experiences
- AI Search Products
- AI Learning Products
- AI Writing Assistants
- AI Productivity Tools
- AI Onboarding and First-Time User Experiences
- AI Feedback and Error Recovery
- Trust, Transparency, and Safety in AI Products

Possible research subjects include ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Duolingo, Khan Academy, Grammarly, Notion AI, NotebookLM, and other AI-powered products.

The analysis should distinguish between observable product behavior, documented product information, and personal evaluation. It should avoid unsupported assumptions about internal product mechanisms.

### Projects

Apply product and UX methods to research, design, prototype, or improve AI-powered experiences.

Potential project areas include:

- Conducting an AI Product Usability Evaluation
- Designing an AI Learning Assistant
- Creating an AI Product Requirements Document
- Prototyping a Conversational Learning Experience
- Redesigning an AI Onboarding Flow
- Improving AI Feedback and Error Recovery
- Developing an AI Product Evaluation Framework
- Testing an AI Feature with User Scenarios

Projects may begin with a real user problem, a product opportunity, or an improvement idea identified through case study analysis.

## Learning Approach

AI Product & UX connects user needs, product decisions, and AI capabilities.

The goal is to understand not only what an AI product can do, but also whether it solves a meaningful problem, how users experience it, and how its design can be improved.

The learning process follows three connected activities:

1. **Learn** product development, user research, and AI UX principles.
2. **Review** real AI products to understand their design decisions, user experience, and limitations.
3. **Build** product concepts, prototypes, and improvements based on user needs and evaluation findings.

This approach develops the ability to evaluate AI products critically and translate user insights into better product experiences.
